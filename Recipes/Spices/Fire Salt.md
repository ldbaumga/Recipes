### Fire Salt

>Notes: Found this recipe on TikTok when searching for ideas on what to use smoked peppers for. I did not use precise measurements when first making this, below is a rough estimate of what was used. 

### Ingredients
- [ ] 1/4 Cup dried pepper powder (I used poblanos, banana peppers, scotch bonnets, and 1 Carolina reaper. I used ~10-12 dried peppers, blended)
- [ ] 1/8th Cup of salt.
- [ ] 1 tsp. Cumin
- [ ] 1 tsp. Garlic Powder
- [ ] 1 tsp. Onion Powder
