### Directions
1. Clean and remove tops of vegetables/peppers (optionally remove seeds)
2. Place empty jar on scale, zero out scale, then fill the jar with the vegies and enough water to convert them. Write down their total weight.
3. Pour water out into separate bowl. Take the total weight of vegies and water and multiply it by 0.02 or 0.03 to get the amount of salt needed.
	1. Vegetable weight + water weight (both in grams) x 0.02-0.03 = Amount of Salt needed in grams
4. Add the salt to the water, stir until dissolved, and pour water back into vegie jar.
5. Add a ramekin or plastic wrap to keep veggies submerged, put jar lid on loosely, and leave on counter anywhere between 3 days to 2 weeks depending on your taste.
	1. Kahm yeast is ok, it will look like a web but if its fuzzy, its mold and needs thrown out
